📝 How to Add Your Signature to Gmail

  1. Click the "Download Signature" button above to save your signature as an image.
  2. Open Gmail and click the gear icon ⚙️ in the top right, then select "See all settings".
  3. Scroll down to the "Signature" section.
  4. Click "+ Create new" and give your signature a name (e.g., "Whisk Signature").
  5. In the signature editor, click the image icon 🖼️ to insert an image.
  6. Upload the signature image you downloaded in step 1.
  7. Once uploaded, resize the image to an appropriate size (recommended: 400-500px width).
  8. Scroll down and click "Save Changes" at the bottom of the page.

💡 Pro Tips:

• Make sure your signature isn't too large - aim for around 400-500 pixels wide for optimal display.

• Test your signature by sending yourself an email to ensure it displays correctly.

• You can set different signatures for new emails vs. replies in Gmail settings.

• For best results, make sure all your contact information is accurate before downloading.
